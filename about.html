About the project

Explore Bangladesh is a tourist spot discovery and travel guide. Visitors can browse by division, district or category, see the exact location on a map, look through photos and video, and read practical instructions for getting there from Dhaka.

An administrator can add, edit and permanently delete tourist spots. Every list, search result, map marker, favorite and count updates straight away.

Good to know

This site stores its data in your browser (localStorage), not in a shared database. Changes made in the admin area are visible on this browser only.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Weather from Open-Meteo.
