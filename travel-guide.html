Getting around Bangladesh

Schedules, fares and access rules change often, so confirm before you leave.

🚌 Bus

The most flexible option. AC coaches serve major routes from Dhaka; book a seat in advance for weekends and holidays, and confirm the boarding counter.

🚆 Train

Intercity trains are comfortable on routes to Sylhet, Chattogram, Rajshahi, Khulna and Rangpur. Tickets are sold on the Bangladesh Railway e-ticket site (eticket.railway.gov.bd) and sell out fast.

✈️ Air

Domestic flights connect Dhaka with Chattogram, Cox's Bazar, Sylhet, Jashore, Barishal, Saidpur, Rajshahi and other airports, and save many hours on long routes.

🛺 Local

CNG auto-rickshaws, rickshaws, ride-share apps in big cities, jeeps in the hills and boats on rivers and haors. Agree the fare before you start.

When to go

Winter (November to February)

Dry, cool and clear. The best season for most beaches, hills, heritage sites and bird watching.

Summer (March to May)

Hot and humid, with occasional storms. Good for cooler hill stations; plan sightseeing early in the day.

Monsoon (June to October)

Green landscapes, full waterfalls, flooded swamp forests and haors. Roads and boats can be disrupted, so allow extra time.

Packing checklist

  • Light cotton clothes, a rain jacket and a hat
  • Comfortable walking shoes with grip
  • Sunscreen, insect repellent and any medicines you need
  • Reusable water bottle and a power bank
  • Cash in small notes; card acceptance is limited outside cities
  • National ID or passport, plus copies

Emergency information

Numbers can change. Verify before you travel.

    Travel responsibly

    Keep places clean

    Carry your rubbish out. Avoid single-use plastic on beaches, in forests and near water.

    Respect local culture

    Dress modestly at religious sites, ask before photographing people, and support local guides and shops.

    Follow local rules

    Follow safety instructions, permits and warnings for forests, borders and sea. Check the weather before you go.
